## 2. The position

Every café on Samui sells coffee. The differentiator is not the room, the
concept or the hours — it is **twenty-three craft sodas at one price**, which is
a thing no other café on this island is doing.

That is also the most *filmable* thing here. Coffee is brown liquid in a cup.
Soda is colour, ice, a pour and fizz — it performs on TikTok and Pinterest in a
way a flat white never will.

**One-liner:** *Twenty-three craft sodas, two prices, and a room built for doing
nothing. Bophut, Koh Samui.*

Supporting facts, all true and all worth leading with:
- Open from **07:30**, earlier than most of Bophut
- **Two prices for the whole menu** — ฿89 and ฿169, nothing to work out
- Site, menu and ordering in **English, Thai and Russian**
- **1 point per ฿50**, redeemable in-store
- Café by day, bar in the evening, same room

## 3. The engine: one flavour a week

Twenty-three flavours is twenty-three weeks of content that writes itself, and
it never runs out until it has gone around once — by which point it's a
returning format, not a repeat.

**Format:** one flavour, shot the same way every time — pour over ice, close,
daylight, no talking over it. Same frame each week builds the recognisable
series; only the colour changes.

**Caption template:**

> `[flavour] — [what it tastes of] · ฿89`
> `คราฟต์โซดา 23 รส ที่บ่อผุด · เปิดทุกวัน 07:30`

Put the flavour poll in stories: *"next week — Yuzu or Ume?"* Whoever votes has
a reason to come in and check that you posted the one they picked.

**Cadence:** 3–4 posts a week.

| Slot | Pillar | What |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Flavour of the week | The pour, 15–20s |
| Wed | Food | One of the nine dishes, ฿169, plated |
| Fri | The room | Door-to-seat, quiet corner, no voiceover |
| Sun *(optional)* | Evening | Low light, records, the bar as a room |

Post 07:30–09:00 and 17:00–19:00 Thailand time — when people are deciding
where to go.

## 6. Two rules that are not style opinions

**Advertise only what the till can ring up.** This folder spent a month
advertising coffee, bowls, breakfasts and five island teas, none of which
existed. Before a claim about the menu goes out, check it against
`https://niksensamui.com/api/public/menu` — that endpoint is the menu, and a
customer who arrives for a thing we do not sell is a bad review we wrote
ourselves.

**Be careful with the bar.** Thailand's amended Alcoholic Beverage Control Act
(No. 2) B.E. 2568 has been in force since 8 Nov 2025 and restricts alcohol
advertising. Say the bar is open and what time it opens. **Do not post drink
prices, brands or promotions** — that is the part that gets fined, and it is
also why nothing alcoholic appears in the online catalogue at all. Worth
confirming with local counsel before any paid placement.

There is one of these live right now: `pinterest/pin-offer.png` advertises
"1 spirit + 1 craft soda · 129฿ · every day, all night" on a public board.
Take it down — see `pinterest/README.md`.

## 8. Open question: nobody knows what time we open

Four published sources give four different answers.

| Source | Opens | Closes |
|---|---|---|
| **The site** (schema.org + every landing string) | **07:30** | 14:00, then 17:00–23:00 |
| Every marketing doc in this folder, before today | 07:00 | 14:00, then 17:00–23:00 |
| `pinterest/pin-menu.png`, live on the board | 08:00 | 13:00 |
| `pinterest/pin-night.png`, live on the board | — | "records till 02:00" |

Everything in this folder has been set to **07:30–14:00 · 17:00–23:00**, because
the site is what a customer reads and it should not disagree with the marketing.
That is a tie-break, not a fact.

**If 07:30 is wrong, the site is wrong too** — and that is the more expensive
error, because it is also in the schema.org block Google reads for the opening
hours in the local pack. Confirm the real hours and the closing time, then say
so: if they differ from 07:30–14:00 / 17:00–23:00 the fix is a code change in
`index.html` and `src/landingStrings.ts` as well as an edit here, plus the
Google Business Profile.

Also unresolved: `pin-night.png` says the room runs to 02:00 and the site says
23:00. Those cannot both be true.
