## How TikTok ranking works (the short version)

TikTok indexes four places, in this order of weight:
1. **Words you speak** in the video (auto-transcribed)
2. **On-screen text** (the native text tool, first 3 seconds matter most)
3. **Caption** (first line = the query you want to rank for)
4. **Hashtags** (least weight — 3–5, never 20)

So: say the keyword, show the keyword, write the keyword. One target query per video.

## Caption formula

`[target query] — [hook in the other language] | [hours or price fact]`

Examples:
- `คาเฟ่ลับบ่อผุด ที่เปิด 7 โมงครึ่ง — the secret bar that's a café before noon 🥤 คราฟต์โซดา 23 รส เปิดทุกวัน 07:30`

## Content pillars (match the brand's two halves of the day)

1. **Morning** (rank: คาเฟ่/breakfast) — the craft-soda pour over ice, a ฿169 dish coming out, slow-morning room shots. One flavour per video, named out loud. Say "คาเฟ่ลับที่บ่อผุด เกาะสมุย" in the first 3 seconds.
2. **Evening** (rank: บาร์ลับ/hidden bar) — low light, records, the room after dark. On-screen text: "secret bar · Bophut". **No drink prices, brands or promotions** — see the alcohol-advertising note below.
3. **The room** (rank: ที่เที่ยว/hidden gem) — door-to-seat walk-in shots, "nothing to do here, that's allowed" mood. These travel furthest; keep the address in the caption.

Cadence: 3–4 posts/week, at least one per pillar. Post mornings 07:30–09:00, evenings 17:00–19:00 Thailand time — TikTok favors posting when your audience is deciding where to go.

## Voice rules (same as the brand doc)

Short, warm, literal. Tell them what it is, then invite them in. "Please come in." /
"เชิญเข้ามาเลย" closes captions the way it closes the window vinyl. No "hidden gem!!"

## Two things that are not style opinions

**Film only what the till can ring up.** The menu is 23 craft sodas at ฿89 and
nine dishes at ฿169 — no coffee, no tea line, no bowls, no breakfast menu. This
playbook used to tell you to film a coffee pour and a tea flight; neither
exists. Check `https://niksensamui.com/api/public/menu` before a menu claim
goes in a caption. Full list in `content-plan.md`.

**Do not put drink prices on the bar videos.** Thailand's amended Alcoholic
Beverage Control Act (No. 2) B.E. 2568 has been in force since 8 Nov 2025 and
restricts alcohol advertising. Show the room, say the hours, name no brands,
prices or promotions. An earlier version of this file told you to post a
priced spirit-and-soda offer, which is exactly the thing that gets fined.
