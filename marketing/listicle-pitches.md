# Getting niksen onto the Koh Samui guides

Organic page 1 for "bar in samui" belongs to aggregators and listicles, not to
venue websites. The way in is to appear *inside* those lists. This is the target
list and the copy to send.

**Order of operations:** the two free listings first (they're certain and take
20 minutes), then the editorial pitches. Editorial pitches land far better with
a venue photo attached, so they're worth holding until after the photo shoot.

---

## 1. TripAdvisor — free listing (do this first)

TripAdvisor holds the top organic result for "bars in Koh Samui". You don't
pitch it, you claim it: add the business yourself at
`tripadvisor.com/Owners`, under Restaurants (it covers cafés and bars).

Nothing to write. Needs: name, address, hours, phone, website, category, photos.
Every review that lands there also feeds the Google local pack indirectly,
because people cross-check.

## Notes

**Say yes to the visit offer.** A writer who has actually sat in the room writes
a better entry than one working from a press release, and it costs you a coffee.
Both editorial pitches above end with that invitation — it's the strongest part
of each.

**Don't claim what you don't have yet.** No "best", no "acclaimed", no review
counts. The hooks that are true and enough on their own: it's new, it opens at
7:00 when little else does, the concept is unusual for Samui, and it runs in
three languages.

**Some "best of" lists are paid.** If a reply comes back quoting a fee, that's a
paid placement, not editorial. Judge it as advertising and price it accordingly.

**Attach photos.** Editors need a hero image. Right now there are dish photos but
no venue or interior shots — worth having a few from the shoot before the
editorial pitches go out. The two free listings can go now and be updated later.

**Keep drinks copy factual.** Thailand's amended Alcoholic Beverage Control Act
(No. 2) B.E. 2568 has been in force since 8 Nov 2025 and restricts alcohol
advertising. These drafts state the service plainly and name no brands, prices or
promotions. Worth confirming with local counsel before any paid placement.
