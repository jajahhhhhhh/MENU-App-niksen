# Getting niksen onto the Koh Samui guides

Organic page 1 for "bar in samui" belongs to aggregators and listicles, not to
venue websites. The way in is to appear *inside* those lists. This is the target
list and the copy to send.

> **The shop has not opened.** Nothing here may say it has. A guide entry is
> very hard to get corrected once published, and an editor who checks the site
> and finds "opening soon" will not run the piece — or will run it with that
> line in it. Send the two free listings as *opening soon* or hold them; hold
> the editorial pitches until there is a door to walk through and a photo of it.

**Order of operations:** the two free listings first (they're certain and take
20 minutes), then the editorial pitches. Editorial pitches land far better with
a venue photo attached, and we still have none — see `shot-list.md` §1. That
shoot and the opening itself are what hold up everything below the line.

**The menu in these drafts is the real one** — 23 craft sodas at ฿89, nine
dishes at ฿169, café by day and bar in the evening. Earlier versions of this
file offered editors specialty coffee, slow breakfasts, bowls and five island
teas. None of that has been on the menu for some time, and a guide entry is
very hard to get corrected once it's published.

## 1. TripAdvisor — free listing (do this first)

TripAdvisor holds the top organic result for "bars in Koh Samui". You don't
pitch it, you claim it: add the business yourself at
`tripadvisor.com/Owners`, under Restaurants (it covers cafés and bars).

Nothing to write. Needs: name, address, hours, phone, website, category, photos.
Every review that lands there also feeds the Google local pack indirectly,
because people cross-check.

## Notes

**Say yes to the visit offer.** A writer who has actually sat in the room writes
a better entry than one working from a press release, and it costs you a soda.
Both editorial pitches above end with that invitation — it's the strongest part
of each.

**Don't claim what you don't have.** No "best", no "acclaimed", no review
counts. The hooks that are true and enough on their own: twenty-three sodas and
no coffee, we open at 07:30 when little else does, the concept is unusual for
Samui, there's an evening that works without alcohol, and it runs in three
languages.

**"Longest soda list on Samui" is a claim, not a fact.** It is very probably
true and nobody will check it, but it is phrased as *as far as we know* in the
Thaiger draft for a reason. Keep it that way.

**Some "best of" lists are paid.** If a reply comes back quoting a fee, that's a
paid placement, not editorial. Judge it as advertising and price it accordingly.

**Attach photos.** All 32 menu items are photographed, and `pinterest/pin-room.png`
is a usable interior — but it's portrait and shot at night, and editors want a
horizontal daylight wide of the room, which we don't have. `shot-list.md` §1 is
the shoot. The two free listings can go now and be updated later; hold the
editorial pitches until you have that wide.

**Keep drinks copy factual.** Thailand's amended Alcoholic Beverage Control Act
(No. 2) B.E. 2568 has been in force since 8 Nov 2025 and restricts alcohol
advertising. These drafts state the service plainly and name no brands, prices
or promotions. Worth confirming with local counsel before any paid placement.
