# niksen secret bar — Launch Campaign Kit
**"13 Days of Doing Nothing"** · 6 → 24 August 2026 · Bophut, Koh Samui

---

## 1. Overview

- **Objective:** Fill the room in opening week — 100+ customers through the door in the first 7 days, 30+ online orders via `/order`, 25+ Google reviews by end of August.
- **Secondary:** 300 Instagram followers, 100 LINE OA friends by 31 Aug (both feed the rainy-season regulars pipeline).
- **One-liner:** *A secret little café where doing nothing is the whole point — opening 18 August in Bophut.*

## 2. Audience

| Segment | Who | Reach them via |
|---|---|---|
| **Tourists (EN)** | Couples & remote workers staying Bophut/Chaweng, hunting "best café koh samui" | Instagram, Google Maps, TripAdvisor later |
| **Russian expats/tourists (RU)** | Large Samui community, very active in Telegram/IG | Instagram RU captions, Google Maps RU reviews |
| **Thai locals & staff of nearby businesses (TH)** | Daily-coffee crowd, delivery at work | LINE OA, Facebook, Grab |

## 3. Key messages

1. **Core:** Niksen is the Dutch art of doing nothing — we built a whole café around it. Slow down, we'll bring the coffee.
2. Real food, honest prices — specialty coffee from ฿60, breakfasts, bowls, five signature island teas at ฿120.
3. Open **every day, 07:00–14:00 & 17:00–23:00** — earlier than the island wakes up.
4. Order ahead at **niksensamui.com/order** — pickup or delivery, points on every ฿50.

*(Voice: calm, warm, a little playful. Never shouty. The brand whispers.)*
